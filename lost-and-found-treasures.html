Lost & Found Treasures is an antique store on Washington Street in Roslindale, stocked with hand-picked knick-knacks, ornamental pieces, and the odd toy collectible that shouldn't have survived this long — but did.

What's inside

Nothing in the shop is arranged to look older than it is, and nothing is sold in worse shape than it deserves. Visitors describe it as small but not cluttered — a tight, well-edited room of ornamental pieces, cultural finds, toy collectibles, and the newer things that earned a place next to them.

Every piece is chosen by hand rather than bought in bulk, which is the difference between a shop full of old junk and a shop full of things someone actually wanted to keep. Regulars carry a loyalty punch card, and the shelves turn over often enough that it's worth a second look every few weeks.

Ways to shop

  • In-store shopping — browse the shelves in person, Wednesday through Sunday.
  • In-store pickup — reserve a find and collect it on your own schedule.
  • Delivery — for the pieces too good, or too heavy, to carry home.
